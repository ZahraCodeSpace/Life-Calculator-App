# 🎀 Life Calculator

**Discover Your Life in Numbers!**

An interactive age calculator that transforms your age into years, months, days, hours, minutes, and seconds with a beautiful and colorful design.

<br/>

## ✨ Features

- 🎂 Calculate age based on birth year
- 📅 Display Gregorian and Persian birth years
- 🗓️ Convert age into years and months
- ☀️ Calculate total days and hours lived
- ⏱️ Display total minutes and seconds
- 🎨 Modern and colorful user interface
- 💜 Beautiful pink, purple, and blue gradients
- ✨ Clean and user-friendly design

## 💡 About The Project

Life Calculator is a frontend project created to practice JavaScript and explore date calculations.

It provides a simple way to visualize lifetime in different time units through an interactive and visually appealing interface.
